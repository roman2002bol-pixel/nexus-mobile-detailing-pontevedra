Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a Full Detail take?

Plan on 2 to 4 hours on-site — vehicle size and how much the interior needs both factor in. We'll narrow that window down when we lock in your booking.

Do I need to be home while you work?

No. Leave the car unlocked or drop keys with us, go about your day, and we'll text you when it's ready.

My car gets a lot of sand and salt air near the beach — does that affect the price?

It might add some time if the carpets are really packed with sand or there's heavy salt film outside — mention it up front and we'll flag any change before we start, never after.
