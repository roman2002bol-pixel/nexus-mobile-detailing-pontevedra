Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I book a Standard Detail?

Every 4–6 weeks is the sweet spot for most people – often enough to stay ahead of salt film and sand, without paying for a wash the car didn't need yet.

Does this include deep stain removal?

It handles everyday spots and light staining. Anything set-in – old coffee, ground-in sand, pet accidents – needs the Interior Only Deep Clean or Full Detail instead.

How long does it take?

Usually an hour to 90 minutes, depending on the car's size and how dirty it actually is.
