Real protection starts with real prep

Ceramic coating is only as good as the surface underneath it. That's why every coating job starts with proper paint correction and polishing — not just a quick wipe and spray. The result is a durable, glossy layer of protection against UV, salt air, and road grime — the three things that age a car fastest a mile from the Atlantic.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does ceramic coating actually help with salt air near the coast?

Yes — a properly applied ceramic coating creates a sealed, hydrophobic layer that sheds salt spray and moisture before it can etch into clear coat, which is a real problem for cars parked near the ocean in Ponte Vedra.

Why does pricing start at $550 instead of a flat rate?

Every vehicle's paint condition is different — some need more correction work than others before coating. We inspect your car first and confirm an exact price before any work begins.

Can this be done in my driveway?

Yes — like all of our services, ceramic coating is applied on-site with our fully equipped mobile unit. We just need a reasonably clean, dry, shaded area to work in.
