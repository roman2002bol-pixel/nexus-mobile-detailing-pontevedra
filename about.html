NEXUS Mobile Detailing was built around one idea: you shouldn't have to rearrange your day to get your car cleaned – and Florida shouldn't have an off-season for it.

Our Story

Why we didn't open a shop

NEXUS started with a simple observation about Ponte Vedra Beach: this is a place where people's time is worth a lot, and their cars are worth even more – so a detailer that makes you drop off a $90,000 SUV at a shop and hope for the best was never going to fit. We built the opposite: a self-contained unit that comes to wherever the car already is, works around your schedule instead of the other way around, and treats a car parked a mile from saltwater differently than one that isn't.

The name is the idea – NEXUS is the point where two things meet. Here, that's your schedule meeting our unit's, and a car's paint meeting conditions most inland detailers never have to think about.
